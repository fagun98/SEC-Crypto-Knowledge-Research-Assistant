## 🔥 Top Stories

### 1. SEC Chair launches “Project Crypto” with CFTC to explore tokenization + innovation exemption framework (2026-02-04, SEC Chair speech)
**Title/Source:** “Keynote Address at AFME’s Annual Financial Services Policy Dinner” (Chair Paul S. Atkins) — (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)

**Key points**
- **FACTS:** The SEC announced **Project Crypto** as a **joint SEC–CFTC initiative** to explore tokenization and develop an **“innovation exemption framework for permissioned on-chain finance.”** (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)  
- **FACTS:** The Chair said the SEC aims to **preserve investor protection, transparency, and supervisory oversight** while modernizing how assets are issued, traded, margined, and settled. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)  
- **ANALYSIS:** Even without immediate rule text, “innovation exemption” language suggests the SEC is exploring **structured pathways** for regulated on-chain market infrastructure—where **custody controls, auditability, and supervisory access** will be core gating items.
- **INFERENCE:** Expect requests for comment, pilot proposals, and “permissioned” designs that make **control/segregation evidence** and **regulator access** easier than in open, pseudonymous environments.

**Implications**
- **Broker-dealers & crypto custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** Project Crypto is framed as modernization with continued **supervisory oversight** (not deregulation). (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Start mapping how BD supervision and customer protection concepts (e.g., controls, surveillance, recordkeeping) would translate to **on-chain settlement and margin** under a permissioned framework.
- **RIAs & investment funds:**
  - **FACTS:** Tokenization exploration is explicitly on the SEC agenda via Project Crypto. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)
  - **ANALYSIS:** If tokenized funds/securities proliferate, advisers will need clearer positions on **custody eligibility** of on-chain instruments and service providers (and what evidence is required to show assets are safeguarded).
- **Banks/trust companies:**
  - **FACTS:** The Chair described modernization affecting issuance, trading, margining, and settlement—areas where banks often provide custody/settlement infrastructure. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Banks/trusts should anticipate being asked to provide **regulated custody wrappers** for tokenized securities, including contractual and operational commitments around **auditability and control**.
- **Crypto-native custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** The Chair emphasized maintaining **investor protection** and **oversight** while exploring tokenization. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/atkins-remarks-afme-020426)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Crypto-native custodians should prepare to evidence “custody-grade” controls in regulator-native terms (e.g., governance, key management, incident response, independent assurance) to participate in permissioned market pilots.

### 2. Commissioner Uyeda: tokenization “with guardrails,” plus Treasury clearing implementation nearing—touching custody/collateral mechanics (2026-02-09, SEC Commissioner speech)
**Title/Source:** “Remarks at the Asset Management Derivatives Forum 2026: Treasuries and Tokenization” (Commissioner Mark Uyeda) — (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)

**Key points**
- **FACTS:** The SEC has approved additional clearing agencies for Treasuries (including approvals cited for CME Securities Clearing Inc. and ICE Clear Credit LLC) and approved FICC rule changes including **collateral-in-lieu** and **agent clearing expansion** to triparty transactions. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
- **FACTS:** The Treasury central clearing standards were adopted in **Dec. 2023**, deadlines were extended in **Feb. 2025**, and the implementation date is described as approaching “within months.” (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
- **FACTS:** On tokenization, the Commissioner emphasized **technology-neutral rules**, openness to **limited pilots/exemptive relief**, and that guardrails like **custody, disclosure, and investor protection** should remain in place when assets “migrate on-chain.” (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
- **ANALYSIS:** For crypto custody providers targeting tokenized securities, the SEC is signaling a path that is **procedurally feasible (pilots/exemptions)** but **substantively demanding** (prove outcomes: custody, disclosure, investor protection).
- **INFERENCE:** Expect increased scrutiny of how tokenization designs handle: (i) who can move assets (control), (ii) what happens in insolvency, and (iii) how collateral/margin is perfected and reconciled.

**Implications**
- **Broker-dealers & crypto custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** The speech flags Treasury clearing implementation and ongoing staff outreach on open questions (e.g., inter-affiliate exemption, extraterritorial application). (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
  - **ANALYSIS:** BDs should pressure-test whether tokenized collateral or on-chain settlement creates knock-on effects for **reserve computations, possession/control concepts, and reconciliation** (even before formal rule changes).
- **RIAs & investment funds:**
  - **FACTS:** The SEC signaled openness to tokenization pilots/exemptive relief while keeping custody guardrails. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Funds considering tokenized instruments should begin diligence on whether their custodians can support **on-chain proofs, tri-party/clearing workflows, and auditable records** acceptable to exam staff.
- **Banks/trust companies:**
  - **FACTS:** The Commissioner highlighted central clearing progress and coordination with other regulators (Treasury/Fed/CFTC/international). (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Banks offering custody for tokenized securities should plan for **cross-border supervisory coordination** and potential demands for **regulator-readable audit trails**.
- **Crypto-native custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** The speech explicitly ties on-chain migration to maintaining **custody** and investor-protection standards. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/uyeda-remarks-asset-management-derivatives-forum-020926)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Treat “technology-neutral” as a warning: novel key schemes, smart contracts, or MPC designs must still satisfy the same control outcomes—documented, tested, and independently assured.

### 3. SEC Investment Management Director encourages pilots/no-action engagement on AI—raising supervision + recordkeeping expectations for client-facing tools (2026-02-03, SEC Division speech)
**Title/Source:** “Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Investment Management” (Brian Daly, Director, Division of Investment Management) — (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)

**Key points**
- **FACTS:** The Division of Investment Management stated it prefers **engagement with industry** rather than immediately issuing prescriptive AI rules, and invited firms to propose **pilots**, request **no-action letters**, or seek **staff guidance**. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
- **FACTS:** Daly suggested reimagining disclosure using **LLMs** to provide interactive, plain-English answers trained on fund documents, while noting related regulatory/supervision questions. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
- **ANALYSIS:** For custody providers (including crypto custodians) deploying AI in onboarding, client support, transaction monitoring, or incident response, this is a signal to build **defensible governance**: human-in-the-loop supervision, audit trails, and clear accountability.
- **INFERENCE:** SEC exam staff may increasingly ask to see AI change-management controls and evidence that AI outputs are **consistent, retained, and reviewable** like other regulated communications/workflows.

**Implications**
- **Broker-dealers & crypto custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** The speech highlights regulatory questions around supervision and how AI outputs are treated. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
  - **ANALYSIS:** If you use AI chat/agents for customer support related to custody, implement **pre-approved scripts/guardrails**, retention of interactions, and escalation paths for exceptions (e.g., withdrawal disputes, alleged unauthorized transfers).
- **RIAs & investment funds:**
  - **FACTS:** The Division invited requests for no-action/staff guidance for AI initiatives. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Advisers using AI for client communications about digital assets should document: (i) who supervises outputs, (ii) how errors are detected, and (iii) how the firm demonstrates fiduciary alignment.
- **Banks/trust companies:**
  - **FACTS:** The speech emphasizes collaboration over immediate prescriptive rulemaking. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
  - **ANALYSIS:** Custody banks using AI in operations should be ready to show **controls, record retention, and vendor oversight**—especially where AI touches reconciliations, instructions processing, or exception handling.
- **Crypto-native custodians:**
  - **FACTS:** While not crypto-specific, the speech’s engagement posture applies broadly to regulated market participants seeking staff input. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
  - **ANALYSIS:** If AI is used in key-management-adjacent workflows (approvals, anomaly detection, withdrawal screening), treat it as a **material control** requiring testing, monitoring, and clear accountability.

(Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/daly-020326-artificial-intelligence-future-investment-management)
